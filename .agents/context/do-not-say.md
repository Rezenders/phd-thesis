# Do Not Say (thesis level)

Guardrails for framing prose: introduction, conclusion, summary, samenvatting,
propositions. Per-paper prohibitions live in `chapters/<name>/do-not-say.md` and
apply equally when a framing chapter summarizes that paper.

## Prohibited Claims

- Do not summarize a chapter's result more strongly than its chapter
  `claim-ledger.md` allows. In particular, PLANTA did not outperform the other
  SUAVE managing subsystems, and its Cámara comparison is limited to a replicated
  final step on a discretized map.
- Do not present the paper-chapters' contributions as solving TACA reusability in
  general. Each answers the research questions partially (introduction wording).
- Do not describe Tactical Retreat results until the chapter contains them.

## Fragile Phrasings

| Avoid | Prefer | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "only few works fully address" without scope | scope to the reviewed literature | gap claims need a bounded search |
| "this thesis proves" | "this thesis shows, in the evaluated scenarios" | evaluations are scenario-bound |
