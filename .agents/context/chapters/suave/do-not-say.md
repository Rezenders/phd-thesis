# SUAVE Do-Not-Say List

- Do not say SUAVE was evaluated or validated on a real AUV. It runs in Gazebo simulation; real-robot use is a design goal.
- Do not say Metacontrol's improvement in SUAVE is statistically significant. No tests were run (20 runs, means and standard deviations only).
- Do not generalize the SUAVE results beyond the single configuration evaluated (300 s, visibility 1.25--3.75, period 80 s, thruster 1 failing at 35 s).
- Do not say SUAVE perceives water visibility or detects the pipeline with real perception; both are mocked.
- Do not say SUAVE performs pipeline inspection; the Inspect Pipeline function is not implemented.
- Do not mix SUAVE's table (20 runs) with ROSA's or PLANTA's SUAVE results; they are separate experiments.
- Do not call SUAVE the only ROS 2 self-adaptive robotics exemplar without "to the best of our knowledge" (the ROSA chapter scopes it that way).
